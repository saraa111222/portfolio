About Me

I’m a penetration tester driven by curiosity about how systems break — and how to make them harder to break. I approach every target with an attacker’s mindset: map the attack surface, find the weak points, and prove the real impact before anyone malicious does.

My work covers reconnaissance, vulnerability assessment, and hands-on exploitation of web applications and networks, following methodologies such as the OWASP Top 10. Every finding is documented with clear evidence, risk rating, and practical remediation steps so teams can fix issues fast.

Security is only valuable when it is understood, so I focus on clear reporting and communication — turning complex technical weaknesses into actionable improvements that strengthen defenses and protect what matters.
